Block Meaw — Support

How to play

Drag the three pieces onto the 8 × 8 board. A full row or column clears. Clear several lines at once, or clear on move after move, for a combo — a combo survives two moves that clear nothing and ends on the third. The game ends when none of your pieces fits.

Questions

How do I get new cats?
Mochi is with you from the start. The others join as your farthest scene grows: scenes 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11 and 14. Tap the cat on the home screen to choose one.
How do scenes change?
As your score passes each milestone, or at once on a 10, 20, 30 … combo. Every new game starts in the Candy scene.
What is the heart for?
A 15-clear combo earns one heart. When you run out of space, use it to clear the three fullest rows and keep playing — once per game.
Is my game saved?
Yes, automatically after every move. Close the app and continue later. Progress lives on this device only; deleting the app deletes it.
How do I turn off the sound or music?
Use the speaker and music-note buttons at the top of the home screen. The game also follows your iPhone's silent switch.
