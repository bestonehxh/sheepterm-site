Block Meaw — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Block Meaw is a puzzle game. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you.

What the game keeps

Your current game, your best scores, your play statistics (games played, best combo, farthest scene), the cat you chose and your sound settings are saved only on your device, so you can continue where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No network connection is used; the game works fully offline.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: support@sheepterm.com
